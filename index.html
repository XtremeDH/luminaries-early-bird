Good to know

Short FAQ

Is there any cancellation policy?

Due to the nature of our summit, we are unable to offer refunds, including for failure to attend, incomplete attendance, late arrival or early departure. We do understand that plans change: you may sell your pass, or carry it over to next year's edition.

Write to summit@mail.luminaries.earth with a short note about your situation and we will do our best to support you.

How do I get there?

By plane: Zadar Airport is the closest, followed by Split Airport.

By car: on the A1 highway, take the Benkovac / Biograd / Pakoštane exit toward Pakoštane / Vrana. Pine Beach Pakoštane is 15 minutes from there.

By boat from Italy: the nearest ferry line is Ancona – Zadar.
